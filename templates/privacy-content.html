Who handles your information

Thomas Property Services handles information supplied through enquiries. Contact thomaspropertyservicesuk@gmail.com or telephone 07410 928465 with questions about your information.

What we collect and why

The quotation form collects your name, at least one contact method, service and description of the work. Postcode, timeframe and additional details are optional. We use this information to respond, discuss suitability and prepare a quotation, including steps you request before entering a contract. This website does not use enquiry details for marketing.

How enquiries are processed

The system is designed to use Cloudflare Pages and Turnstile for hosting and spam protection, Resend for transactional email and the published Gmail mailbox for correspondence. Submission is unavailable until configured. With an email address, an automatic acknowledgement is requested. Telephone-only enquiries do not receive one.

Cloudflare processes technical information for spam protection. The application uses a salted IP-address hash for short-lived attempt counters. D1 stores random request identifiers, salted request fingerprints, processing states and timestamps for duplicate prevention. Names, contact details and enquiry text are not stored in that database.

Retention

Application attempt counters become eligible for deletion within 20 minutes and duplicate-prevention records after seven days. Expired records are removed when the enquiry service is next used; physical deletion may be later if the service is inactive. Provider logs and backups may follow different schedules. Enquiry content is held by the email services and business mailbox. Before activation the operator must confirm a specific retention period or documented criteria for unsuccessful enquiries and accepted-work records, provider retention and any international-transfer safeguards.
